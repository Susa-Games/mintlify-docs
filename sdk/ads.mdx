---
title: "Ads"
description: "Show rewarded and interstitial ads, and grant rewards only when the platform credits them"
---

Your game asks the SusaPlay page to show an ad, and the page answers when the ad ends. The SDK
never loads an ad itself: which network runs, and whether a reward is credited, is decided by the
platform from your game's ad settings.

## Rewarded ads

```csharp
var result = await SusaPlaySDK.Ads.ShowRewarded();

if (result.Rewarded)
{
    // Credited by the platform (held for a guest until they sign in). Update your UI.
}
else if (result.Success)
{
    // The ad played, but no reward was credited this time.
}
else
{
    Debug.Log($"No ad: {result.Reason}");
}
```

<Warning>
Check `Rewarded`, not `Success`, before you grant anything. An ad can play to the end and still
credit nothing, because the daily maximum and the cooldown are checked on the server after the ad
finishes.
</Warning>

The platform credits the reward to the player's wallet itself. Your game never adds coins for a
rewarded ad.

## Interstitial ads

```csharp
var result = await SusaPlaySDK.Ads.ShowInterstitial();
```

An interstitial credits nothing, so `Rewarded` is always `false` and `Success` is the only answer
that matters.

## AdResult

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Success` | `bool` | The ad played to the end. Says nothing about a reward |
| `Rewarded` | `bool` | The reward was credited. Always `false` for an interstitial |
| `Reason` | `string` | Why the ad failed, or `null` on success |
| `AdType` | `string` | `rewarded` or `interstitial` |
| `RequestId` | `string` | An id for this request |

| `Reason` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `ADS_DISABLED` | Ads are turned off in your game's ad settings |
| `TIMEOUT` | The SDK had no answer after 180 seconds |
| anything else | Reported by the ad network the platform chose |

## Reward rules

Your game's ad settings set the reward amount, how many rewarded ads a player can be credited for
each day, and how long they wait between rewards. When they are not set, the platform uses 50
coins, 10 a day, and 30 minutes apart. Inside the cooldown or past the daily maximum, the ad still
plays and `Rewarded` is `false`.

While rewarded ads are turned off, every ad request fails with `ADS_DISABLED` — interstitials
included.

Your game's ad settings are managed by SusaPlay. Contact us to change them.

## Test in the Editor

The [Editor Simulator](/sdk/editor-simulator#purchases-and-ads) answers ads in Play Mode using your
game's real ad settings, so you can test the daily maximum and the cooldown without a build.

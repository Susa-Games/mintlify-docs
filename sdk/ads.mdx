---
title: "Ads"
description: "Show rewarded and interstitial ads, and grant your game's reward when a rewarded ad is watched"
---

Your game asks the SusaPlay page to show an ad, and the page answers when the ad ends. The SDK
never loads an ad itself: the platform picks the network from your game's ad settings.

## Rewarded ads

```csharp
var result = await SusaPlaySDK.Ads.ShowRewarded();

if (result.Rewarded)
{
    // Watched to the end. Grant your game's reward: an extra life, a doubled score.
}
else
{
    Debug.Log($"No reward: {result.Reason}");
}
```

`Rewarded` is `true` when the player watched the rewarded ad to the end. Grant your game's own
reward on it. It does not depend on the player being signed in, or on any platform limit.

## Platform coins

Separately from your game's reward, SusaPlay gives a signed-in player a few platform coins for
each rewarded ad. These are the platform's reward, not your game's:

- SusaPlay sets the amount, the daily limit and the cooldown, the same for every game.
- A guest earns no coins.
- The platform has already put the coins in the player's wallet. Never add coins for an ad
  yourself.

`CoinsAwarded` reports how many coins this ad paid, or `0`. Use it only to show the player a
message.

<Note>
Before SDK 1.10.0, `Rewarded` meant the platform had credited coins, and was `false` at the
platform's daily limit or in its cooldown. Update to 1.10.0 so your game's reward follows the
watched ad.
</Note>

## Interstitial ads

```csharp
var result = await SusaPlaySDK.Ads.ShowInterstitial();
```

An interstitial rewards nothing, so `Rewarded` is always `false` and `Success` is the only answer
that matters.

## AdResult

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Success` | `bool` | The ad played to the end |
| `Rewarded` | `bool` | A rewarded ad was watched to the end. Always `false` for an interstitial |
| `CoinsAwarded` | `int` | Platform coins credited for this ad, or `0`. SDK 1.10.0 and later |
| `Reason` | `string` | Why the ad failed, or `null` on success |
| `AdType` | `string` | `rewarded` or `interstitial` |
| `RequestId` | `string` | An id for this request |

| `Reason` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `ADS_DISABLED` | This ad type is turned off in your game's ad settings |
| `TIMEOUT` | The SDK had no answer after 180 seconds |
| anything else | Reported by the ad network the platform chose |

Rewarded ads and interstitials are turned on and off separately. A request for a type that is
turned off fails with `ADS_DISABLED`; the other type keeps working.

Your game's ad settings are managed by SusaPlay. Contact us to change them.

## Test in the Editor

The [Editor Simulator](/sdk/editor-simulator#purchases-and-ads) answers ads in Play Mode with the
platform's coin rules, so you can check that your game rewards a watched ad whether or not coins
are paid.
